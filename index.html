❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is TextHumanify?
TextHumanify is a free online tool that transforms AI-generated text into natural, human-like writing. It removes robotic patterns and makes your content sound more authentic and conversational.
How does the text humanization work?
Our tool uses smart text analysis to identify common AI writing patterns — like overly formal phrases, stiff transitions, and repetitive structures — then replaces them with more natural alternatives. It supports English, Chinese, and Japanese text.
Is my text stored or shared?
No! Your privacy is our priority. All text processing happens locally in your browser. We never store, log, or share your content with anyone.
What languages are supported?
Currently, TextHumanify supports English, Chinese, and Japanese. The tool automatically detects the language and applies the appropriate humanization strategy.
Can I use humanized text for SEO?
Yes, many users humanize AI-generated content to make it more readable and engaging. Our tool helps remove common "AI detection" patterns while keeping your message intact. Always review and edit the output to match your brand voice.
How is this different from AI rewriting tools?
Unlike AI rewriting tools that generate new content, TextHumanify refines your existing text by removing robotic patterns. It keeps your original meaning while making it sound more natural and human-written.
Is there a character limit?
There's no strict limit, but we recommend processing text under 5,000 characters for best results. For longer content, you can process it in sections.
How can I get better results?
For best results: (1) Use complete sentences rather than fragments, (2) Avoid mixing too many languages in one text, (3) Review the output and humanize again if needed for more variation.
